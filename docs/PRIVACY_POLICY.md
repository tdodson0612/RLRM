# RLRM privacy policy

Effective 2026-09-20. RLRM is a free, unofficial fan project.

## The short version
RLRM does not collect, send or share any information about you. Everything
stays on your device.

## What the app stores on your device
- Your rank, preferred mode and focus areas, chosen during setup
- Your progress: skill statuses, ticked checkboxes, favorites, saved sessions and a practice log
- Your settings: button choices and light or dark appearance

This data lives in the app's private storage on your phone. Deleting the app
deletes it, and Settings > Reset progress clears your progress.

## What the app does not do
- It has no accounts, sign-in or profiles.
- It has no analytics, advertising or tracking.
- It does not use the internet. It works fully offline.
- It does not use your camera, photos, microphone, contacts or location.

If a future version adds an optional feature that reads a photo or screenshot of
your game stats, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and
the image will be read on your device only.

## Children
RLRM collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Third parties
RLRM contains no third-party services. It bundles a typeface (Atkinson
Hyperlegible, SIL Open Font License) and open-source code libraries, all listed
under Settings > Open-source licenses.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
